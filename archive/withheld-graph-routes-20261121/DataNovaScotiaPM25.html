Ambient fine particulate matter (PM2.5) at Lake Major

Explore hourly ambient PM2.5 measurements recorded at the Lake Major station from 2006 through 2025. The official record identifies the timestamp field as a floating timestamp, the average field as numeric, and the reported unit as micrograms per cubic metre. Its usage notes describe invalid-monitor status codes and a mass-concentration alignment factor applied beginning January 2, 2024; those notes do not establish how individual observations should be filtered.
