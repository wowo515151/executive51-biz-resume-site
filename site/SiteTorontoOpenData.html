Datasets

Available graphs

  • Toronto's Dashboard Key Indicators

    Explore key indicator data shared with Toronto's City Manager's Office by City divisions and agencies. The dataset includes recent and historical periods used to assess trends for Toronto and its services. Consult the official record for the dashboard context, update schedule, and field descriptions.

  • Bicycle and Micromobility Cordon Count

    Review bicycle and micromobility counts and classifications collected in Toronto's 2022 cordon count study. The observations describe counted travel across the study locations and provide a snapshot for comparing mobility patterns. Check the official record for collection methods, limitations, and available formats.

  • Toronto Open Data Quality Scores

    Explore Toronto’s catalogue-scorecard results and the scoring models from the portal’s first Data Quality Scoring iteration. The scores summarize dataset quality characteristics such as usability, timeliness, completeness, and description quality. Review the official record for newer score files, explanation codes, and update details.
