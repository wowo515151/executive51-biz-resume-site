Données Québec

Québec population estimates and projections

Explore annual population values for Québec health and social service territories. The source presents estimates for 2001–2025 and projections for 2026–2051. Records are organized by territory, year, data type, and sex, with total population available in the TousLesAges field. The source describes the series as supporting both retrospective and forward-looking analysis; interpret projected values according to the official methodology.

View official dataset record: Données Québec

Source: Ministère de la Santé et des Services sociaux; population data produced by the Institut de la statistique du Québec. Official dataset record. Reused under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0.
