Québec municipal financial profile, 2025 data

Browse 2025 municipal financial profile records prepared by Québec’s Ministère des Affaires municipales et de l’Habitation. The rows include municipality names, population, population class, and coded financial measures derived from property assessment summaries. The chart keeps the source’s measure codes visible so they can be checked against the ministry’s accompanying field descriptions before interpretation.
