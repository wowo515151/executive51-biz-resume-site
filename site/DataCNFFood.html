Foods in the Canadian Nutrient File

Browse the food descriptions maintained in Health Canada's Canadian Nutrient File, a reference database for foods commonly consumed in Canada. Each record supplies a food code and food name, which can help identify the entries used in the nutrient resources. Use the official guide to review the database scope and field definitions before interpreting food codes.
