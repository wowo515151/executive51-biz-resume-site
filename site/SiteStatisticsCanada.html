Available graphs

  • Population estimates

    Explore quarterly population estimates for Canada. This view follows the total population series across the latest 100 reference quarters, with counts recorded at each quarter end. Use the official table page to review its coverage, definitions, and available breakdowns.

  • Labour force characteristics

    Explore monthly employment for people aged 15 and over in Canada. This view uses the total-gender seasonally adjusted series and shows the latest 100 reference months. The graph converts Statistics Canada's reported thousands into people using the table's scalar factor. The official table also offers other measures and groups; consult its definitions before comparing them.

  • Consumer Price Index

    Explore Canada's monthly Consumer Price Index for all items across the latest 100 reference months. The index follows price change for a representative basket of consumer goods and services. Review the official table for geography, product coverage, and index-period details before comparing series.
