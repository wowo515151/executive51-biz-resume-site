03Buyer guide

What to ask before hiring a managed AI provider

Define the work and ownership

Describe the result you want, the systems the agent can access, who approves its work and who handles errors. Ask what you receive when the contract ends.

Price the whole service

Request setup fees, recurring fees, usage charges, human review costs and minimum commitments. Confirm the currency and how successful delivery will be measured.

Agree on operation and access

Ask who monitors the system, how incidents are handled and what support hours apply. Confirm data storage, permissions, backups and a workable handover plan.

04FAQ

Alberta managed AI services: common questions

What does “managed AI” mean in this directory?

A provider takes an ongoing role in running, monitoring, maintaining or improving an AI service. That could be a voice agent, a business workflow, a research agent or an enterprise AI system. The agreement still needs to specify exactly which responsibilities the provider takes on.

How is ongoing support different from managed operation?

An implementation company may build an AI system and offer help afterward, while you remain responsible for operating it. Those listings carry the “Implementation + support” label. Use the service-model filter to show only providers with explicit managed-operation evidence.

Are all these companies headquartered in Alberta?

Each listing has evidence of an Alberta base or office from an official website or company-controlled profile. Some also operate elsewhere. The location shown is the company’s published Alberta presence, and the source is linked in its profile. A page that merely says a company serves Alberta was not enough for inclusion.

Can I compare prices directly?

Only after checking the billing basis and scope. An agent-hour, a human consulting hour, a monthly subscription and a fixed project are different things. Ask about usage allowances, setup, oversight, support and taxes before comparing totals.

Does being listed mean Executive51 recommends the company?

Listings document published service offerings and local presence. They are not performance tests, reviews or endorsements. Executive51 is the publisher and a listed service provider; its own listing is labelled. Other companies have not paid for placement in this directory.

05Methodology

Sources and selection methodology

The directory was researched on November 24, 2026. It includes 78 providers with managed-operation evidence and 22 with implementation and ongoing-support evidence. The classification reflects the specific published offering, rather than every service a company may provide.

  • Alberta presence: a stated Alberta base, headquarters or office. Serving Alberta without evidence of a local presence does not qualify.
  • Ongoing AI scope: a published AI service with continued operation, monitoring, maintenance, optimization or support. General software development and generic IT maintenance alone do not qualify.
  • Company sources: each profile links to service and location evidence from official websites or company-controlled profiles. Quotes are short excerpts; open the source for context. Service claims have not been independently performance-tested.
  • Deduplication: renamed companies and multiple offices are combined. Product brands are identified with their operator where the relationship is published.
