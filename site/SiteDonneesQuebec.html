Datasets

Available graphs

  • Québec municipal population and area

    Compare population and land area for Québec municipalities, with regional and MRC context. The source register is updated continuously, while its population figures are based on provincial decrees using estimates from the Institut de la statistique du Québec. The graph request asks only for municipality, population, area, region, and MRC fields; contact details are not requested.

  • Québec population estimates and projections

    Explore comparable population estimates and projections for Québec health and social service territories from 2001 to 2051. Records are organized by territory, year, data type, and sex, with total population available in the TousLesAges field. The source describes the series as supporting both retrospective and forward-looking analysis; interpret projected values according to the official methodology.

  • Québec municipal financial profile, 2025 data

    Browse 2025 municipal financial profile records prepared by Québec’s Ministère des Affaires municipales et de l’Habitation. The rows include municipality names, population, population class, and coded financial measures derived from property assessment summaries. The chart keeps the source’s measure codes visible so they can be checked against the ministry’s accompanying field descriptions before interpretation.
